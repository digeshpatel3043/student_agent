# Northfield Institute of Management - Student Handbook (SAMPLE, FICTIONAL)

Applies to PGDM batch 2025-27. All amounts are in INR. This document is sample data created for the Agentic AI Engineering assignment.

## GEN-1 | Working days and office hours
Working days are Monday to Friday, excluding public holidays declared by the Institute. The Accounts Office, Academic Office and Student Support Desk are open from 10:00 to 17:00 on working days and are closed on Saturdays, Sundays and declared holidays. The phrase "next working day" in any policy means the next such day on which the relevant office is open.

## FEE-1 | Fee schedule and payment modes
Tuition fee is invoiced term by term and every invoice shows a due date. Fees can be paid through the online payment portal, by NEFT/RTGS to the Institute bank account, or by demand draft at the Accounts Office. A payment counts as received on the date the amount is credited to the Institute account. Portal payments are credited on the same day.

## FEE-2 | Late fee
If a term fee is not received by the due date, a late fee of INR 200 per calendar day applies from the day after the due date, up to a maximum of INR 5,000 per term. If dues remain unpaid 15 days after the due date, term registration and the examination hall ticket are put on hold until the dues and the late fee are cleared.

## FEE-3 | Payment portal failure
If the payment portal fails, or a payment attempt failed, on or before the due date, the student must (1) take a screenshot showing the error with date and time and note any transaction reference, and (2) raise a support ticket under the category "Fee/Payment" within 24 hours of the failure, attaching the screenshot. If the Accounts Office verifies the failure, the student may pay by the next working day without any late fee. Failures that are not reported within 24 hours, or cannot be verified, attract the normal late fee. If money was debited but not credited, the Accounts Office reconciles it within 5 working days and the student should not pay a second time.

## FEE-4 | Instalments and extensions
A term fee can be paid in two instalments only with prior written approval from the Accounts Office. Requests must be submitted at least 7 days before the due date. One due-date extension of up to 15 days is allowed per academic year on payment of a processing fee of INR 1,000. Late fee does not apply during an approved extension.

## FEE-5 | Refund on withdrawal from the programme
If a student withdraws before the programme starts, the tuition fee is refunded after deducting a processing fee of INR 25,000. After the programme has started, the tuition fee is not refundable. Approved refunds are made to the original payment account within 30 days. This section covers tuition fee only.

## SCH-1 | Scholarships: types and eligibility
The Institute offers two scholarships. The Merit Scholarship gives a tuition waiver of 25% or 50% to students with a CGPA of 8.0 or above at the end of Term 2 and attendance of at least 90%. The Need-Based Scholarship gives a tuition waiver of up to 30% to students whose annual family income is below INR 8 lakh and who submit income proof. Applications are made through a support ticket under the category "Scholarship" with the application form attached. Decisions are announced within 21 days of a complete application.

## SCH-2 | Scholarship conditions and renewal
A scholarship is applied as an adjustment on the next term fee invoice and is never paid out in cash. To keep a scholarship the student must maintain a CGPA of at least 7.5 and attendance of at least 85% in every term. If either condition is not met, the scholarship is suspended for the following term and can be reinstated once the condition is met again. A student can hold only one scholarship at a time.

## CRS-1 | Changing specialisation
A student may change specialisation (for example from Big Data Analytics to AI & Data Science) only once during the programme. The request must be submitted before the end of the second week of a term, using the Specialisation Change Form attached to a support ticket under the category "Course Change". The Programme Chair decides based on seat availability and academic record, and the decision is communicated within 5 working days. There is no change in tuition fee.

## CRS-2 | Elective add and drop
Electives can be added or dropped during the first 7 days of a term through the student portal. After this window any change needs written approval from the Programme Chair. A student must remain registered for at least 18 credits in every term.

## ATT-1 | Attendance
A minimum attendance of 75% in each course is required to appear for end-term examinations. Students with attendance between 70% and 74.9% may apply for condonation with a valid reason and a fee of INR 1,000. Medical absences need a doctor's certificate submitted within 7 days of returning.

## SUP-1 | Support tickets and escalation
Support requests are raised as tickets in one of these categories: Fee/Payment, Scholarship, Course Change, Academic Records, IT/Portal, Other. Normal tickets receive a first response within 2 working days and urgent fee-related tickets within 1 working day. If a ticket is not answered in this time the student can escalate to the Student Affairs Office at studentaffairs@northfield.example.

## DOC-1 | Certificates and documents
Bonafide certificates, fee receipts and transcripts are requested through a support ticket under the category "Academic Records". Bonafide certificates and fee receipts are issued within 3 working days and transcripts within 7 working days.
